### Subspaces and dimension

I develop the relationship between bases, spanning, and linear independence, then use basis extension to explain the dimension formula for the sum and intersection of subspaces. The accompanying discussion examines when the union of two subspaces is itself a subspace.

### Inequalities and orthogonal decomposition

I present a vector-based derivation of the Cauchy–Bunyakovskii–Schwarz inequality using orthogonal decomposition. I then connect the result to a bound on the square of a sum, showing how an abstract inequality can be used in a concrete calculation.

### Norms and Vandermonde matrices

I work through Euclidean norm calculations and explain the connection between Vandermonde matrices, polynomial roots, and linear independence. Page 7 continues the preceding norm calculation; the PDF retains my intermediate handwritten steps.

### Fourier representations and norm geometry

I discuss a Fourier matrix and a discrete-time Fourier-transform expression, then illustrate p-norms through the geometry of their unit spheres and the limiting infinity norm. The sketches accompany the formulas to support geometric interpretation.
