## About this collection

The repository contains a 12-page PDF of annotated linear algebra recitation material. It combines selected source excerpts with handwritten derivations, geometric sketches, and worked explanations used in teaching. It is a sample of my recitation work, not a complete textbook or course archive.

[Open the annotated recitation PDF](linear-algebra-recitation.pdf)

## Topics and page guide

| Pages | Topics |
| --- | --- |
| 1–3 | Bases, linear independence, spanning sets, and the dimension formula for the sum and intersection of subspaces; a proof using basis extension; when a union of subspaces is a subspace |
| 4–6 | Cauchy–Bunyakovskii–Schwarz inequality; orthogonal decomposition; a vector-based proof and an application to bounding the square of a sum |
| 7–9 | Euclidean norm calculations and Vandermonde matrices; interpreting linear independence through roots of a polynomial |
| 10–12 | Fourier matrix and a discrete-time Fourier-transform expression; p-norms; geometric interpretation of unit spheres; the limiting infinity norm |

Page 7 is a short continuation of the preceding norm calculation. The document preserves the original working-note format, including handwritten intermediate steps and annotations.

## Teaching approach illustrated

The notes combine proof development with geometric and numerical examples. Topics include extending a basis to establish the dimension formula, deriving inequalities through orthogonal decomposition, interpreting a Vandermonde system through polynomial roots, and connecting norm formulas to geometric shapes.

The supplied classroom screenshots support the context of an online recitation on 18 May 2026. They are not published here because they include student names, chat messages, and classroom access information. Private planning messages are also excluded.
